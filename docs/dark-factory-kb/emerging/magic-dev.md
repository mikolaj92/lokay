- **Funding:** Large rounds (reports: $320M+ tranche with Schmidt/CapitalG/Atlassian/Sequoia/etc.; site cites $515M total and thousands of GB200s). High valuation / small headcount narratives common in 2024–25 coverage.

- **MagicAgent (academic name collision risk):** arXiv papers titled “MagicAgent” may be unrelated third-party work — don’t conflate with Magic.dev without checking authors.
